Before the study

Informed Consent

Please read the following information carefully before deciding whether to participate.

Study information

You are invited to participate in a research study on human–AI interaction.

You will be asked to complete a pre-study questionnaire and then take part in a collaborative game with an AI. In the game, you will collaborate with the AI to capture demons accidentally summoned by a wizard by selecting the appropriate spells for each demon. After completing the game, you will be asked to complete a post-study questionnaire.

Research team

Prof. Rui Prada, Dr Carla Pacheco, Dr Filipa Correia and Margarida Serralheiro.

INESC-ID and Instituto Superior Técnico, Universidade de Lisboa.

Study objective and data processing purpose

The purpose of this study is to investigate human–AI collaboration in a game-based context and to collect quantitative and qualitative data about participants’ experiences during the collaboration.

Who can participate

Participants must be at least 18 years old and proficient in English.

Voluntary participation

Your participation is voluntary, and you can withdraw from the study at any time. There will be no penalty if you decide to withdraw.

Procedure

Participation will take approximately 20 minutes and consists of:

  1. A short questionnaire before the task.
  2. An interactive game involving an AI team member.
  3. A short questionnaire after the task.

Risks

The risks of participating in this study are not greater than what you would expect in your daily life.

Confidentiality and data retention

All data collected during the study will be de-identified. The information will be securely stored on an INESC-ID server and identified only by a numerical identifier to prevent association with personal data.

The collected data will be retained for two years following the conclusion of the study, in accordance with institutional policies and applicable legal requirements. After this period, all data will be securely deleted or irreversibly anonymised. Anonymised data may be retained for scientific and academic purposes, including publications, while ensuring that no participant can be identified.

Data controller

The entity responsible for data processing is INESC-ID — Institute for Systems and Computer Engineering: Research and Development, Rua Alves Redol 9, Lisbon, Portugal. The research team will access the data for the purposes of this project.

Data protection will be overseen by the INESC-ID Data Protection Officer at dpo@inesc-id.pt.

Legal basis and purpose of processing

Your data will be processed based on your consent, which can be withdrawn at any time. The data will be processed to explore levels of performance in human–AI collaboration and will be kept until the end of the project, subject to the retention conditions described above.

Recipients and international transfers

Your data may be shared with other universities and research institutes involved in the study. No international data transfers are expected.

Your rights

You have the right to ask the data controller for access to your personal data, as well as its rectification, erasure or restriction of processing. Where applicable, you also have the right to object to processing and the right to data portability. You may also submit a complaint to the Portuguese National Data Protection Commission.

Questions and contacts

You are free to ask questions at any time during the experiment if you have any concerns. After the experiment, you may contact Margarida Serralheiro at margarida.serralheiro@inesc-id.pt or Rui Prada at rui.prada@inesc-id.pt.
